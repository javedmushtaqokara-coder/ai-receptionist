# Hair salon

Last checked: 2026-11-25. Sources: Claude's industry knowledge of salon
front-desk calls, plus the first client's site (perfectxhair.com, a
four-location Korean salon in Toronto), whose policies (deposits,
48-hour cancellation, confirmation requests) are typical of busy salons. Add
to this file on later salon builds.

## 1. What calls sound like

- **Who calls:** existing clients rebooking with their stylist (the
  biggest group), new clients after Instagram, TikTok or Google, people
  running late, people changing or cancelling, product or online-shop
  questions, gift cards, job seekers, vendors.
- **Mood:** friendly and casual, sometimes rushed ("I'm running late!").
  New clients are nervous about colour or perm results and price.
- **Words callers use:** "trim", "layers", "bangs", "balayage",
  "highlights", "root touch-up", "toner", "bleach", "colour correction",
  "Korean perm", "digital perm", "down perm", "straightening",
  "keratin", "blowout", "up-do", "with Lila / with my usual stylist",
  "how long does it take", "is there a deposit".
- **Sample openings:**
  1. "Hi, can I book a haircut with Leah this weekend?"
  2. "How much is a digital perm, and how long does it take?"
  3. "I need to move my appointment tomorrow."
  4. "I'm running about 15 minutes late."
  5. "Do you do balayage on dark hair? I've never had it done."

## 2. Call mix

| Reason | Share |
|---|---|
| Book (often with a specific stylist) | 40–50% |
| Reschedule, cancel, running late, confirm | 20–25% |
| Price, duration and service questions | 15–20% |
| Location, hours, parking | 5–10% |
| Products, gift cards, complaints or redo requests, jobs | the rest |

## 3. What callers ask, and what a good answer needs

- **Price:** "from" prices ("$70 and up"). The final price depends on
  length, density and condition, and the stylist confirms before starting.
- **Duration:** needed for booking. Long chemical services run 3–5 hours.
- **Which service:** colour with or without bleach, balayage vs highlights,
  which perm. Never decide; suggest a consultation with the stylist, or
  book the most likely service and note it.
- **Policies:** deposits, cancellation notice, late arrival, no-shows,
  payment methods, extensions, children's age limits.
- **Stylists:** who is at which location, specialities, and new versus
  senior stylist pricing.

## 4. What gets booked

- Service (or the service group), location, preferred stylist, date and
  time, name, phone, email.
- Combined services (cut plus colour) take longer. Long sessions often need
  a deposit, which is taken by the booking app.
- Most salons use Vagaro, Fresha, Square, Boulevard, GlossGenius,
  Mindbody, Booksy or Phorest. Booking outside that system risks double
  booking a stylist's chair.

## 5. What gets transferred or escalated

- An unhappy client or redo request (within the policy window): take
  details, and the manager calls back.
- Colour correction and major transformations: these need a consultation.
- Billing disputes, deposit refunds, no-show fees.
- Wholesale, partnerships, press.

## 6. Emergencies and after hours

There are no real emergencies. After hours: book online slots, take
messages, and handle cancellations, noting the cancellation notice policy.
A late-arrival call outside hours just needs a message for the salon.

## 7. Rules and risks

- Never promise an exact price, a colour result, or that a service suits
  someone's hair. The stylist decides at consultation.
- Never waive deposits or cancellation fees.
- Allergy or sensitivity questions: suggest a patch test or a consultation
  with the stylist; never give medical advice.
- Respect stylist privacy: don't give out personal numbers or schedules
  beyond what's bookable.
