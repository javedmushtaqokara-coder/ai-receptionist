### Services

- **Kitchens:** custom and semi-custom cabinetry; quartz, marble, granite and quartzite counters; tile and backsplash; islands; appliance installation; lighting and electrical.
- **Bathrooms:** custom tile and stone; walk-in showers and wet rooms with frameless glass; freestanding and soaking tubs; vanities; plumbing and fixtures (rain showers, body jets, heated towel bars); lighting, backlit mirrors and heated floors; waterproofing.
- **Full home:** combined kitchen and bath; full interior renovation; custom millwork and built-ins; flooring throughout; electrical and plumbing upgrades; open-concept conversions (structural, beams); permits and project management; condo and penthouse buildouts.

### Process

1. Free in-home consultation, no obligation.
2. Design and material selection, with a detailed itemized quote (floor plans and 3D renderings for full homes).
3. Permits and prep: Miami-Dade, City of Miami, HOA and condo boards.
4. Build and install.
5. Final walk-through; the warranty starts at sign-off.
