### Policies

- **Confirmations:** the booking system asks for confirmation 72 hours ahead. If an appointment isn't confirmed 24 hours before, it may be cancelled after they try to reach the client.
- **Deposits:** $50 for 3-hour sessions and $100 for 5-hour sessions (through the scheduling app).
- **Cancellations:** 48 hours' notice. Later cancellations may be charged a late fee on top of the non-refundable deposit. Cancel by phone, in the app, or in person.
- **No-shows:** the first forfeits the deposit. After that, 50% of the missed service must be paid before rebooking.
- **Late:** over 10 minutes late may mean a shortened service. Over 15 minutes without calling may count as a no-show.
- **Refunds:** none for completed services. Satisfaction guarantee: contact them within 7 days; corrections within 30 days.
- **Payment:** credit and debit only, **no cash**.
- **Extensions:** no services on hair with extensions; they must be removed first.
- **Hair types:** all types welcome. Extra length, density or texture may cost more, and the stylist confirms before starting.
