### Prices published on the site (as DFW typical ranges, not a fixed price list)

- Diagnostic visit (weekday): $89–$149. Some companies credit it toward the repair.
- After-hours premium: $150–$250 off hours (nights, weekends, holidays); "disclosed before dispatch, never after".
- Common repairs: $350–$800 (capacitor $180–$400, contactor $200–$450, condenser fan motor $350–$800, blower motor $450–$1,200, refrigerant recharge $200–$600, evaporator coil $1,200–$2,800, compressor $1,400–$2,800).
- Full AC replacement installed: $5,000–$12,000 (3 ton $5,000–$7,500; 4 ton $6,500–$9,500; 5 ton $8,000–$12,000; heat pump $7,500–$14,000).
- Manual J load calculation: free with any estimate.
- Permits: $75–$300 depending on city.

### Site FAQ answers (from the services page)

- **Unit size:** a 2,000–2,500 sq ft DFW home typically needs 3.5–5 tons. Correct sizing comes from a free Manual J calculation with any estimate.
- **Cost:** roughly $89–149 diagnostic, $350–800 common repair, $5,000–12,000 replacement; Cowboy Air quotes before work begins.
- **Repair vs replace:** plan for 10–15 years in Texas. If a repair costs over 40% of a new system and the unit is over 10 years old, replacing usually wins.
- **How often to service:** twice a year (cooling tune-up in spring, heating check in fall).
- **How fast:** same-day service for calls during business hours, and 24-hour emergency availability. Dispatch confirms the arrival window.
